How do we work?

We work to reduce cognitive friction by combining science and entertainment.

How?

  • Built for your audience

    We design every experience around your audience's needs, context and constraints, making sure it truly fits them.

  • Easy to digest

    We distil the information down to the essentials, so it's easier to understand, process and remember.

  • On-Brand!

    We bring your brand identity to life in every detail, because a learning experience builds your brand too.

And through an agile process, we transfer your knowledge to your audience using a hybrid model that blends human expertise and artificial intelligence.

Powered by hybrid AI

We use AI to speed up analysis and production, and human judgement to make every experience truly tailor-made.
