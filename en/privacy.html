This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, what we use it for and what rights you have, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Data Protection and the Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD).

2. What data we process

  • Contact form data: your name, email address and phone number, plus anything you tell us when we reply.
  • Technical visit data: the provider that hosts the website may log data such as your IP address to keep the service secure.

We do not ask for special categories of data. If you give us someone else's data, you must have their permission.

3. What we use your data for

  • To reply to your contact request.
  • If you ask us to, to prepare a proposal or send you information about our services.

We do not build profiles or make automated decisions, and we will not send you marketing messages without your consent.

4. Legal basis

  • Your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form (Article 6.1.a GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, without affecting any processing carried out before.
  • Taking steps before entering into a contract, when you ask us for a proposal (Article 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping the website secure, for technical visit data (Article 6.1.f GDPR).

5. How long we keep your data

We keep it for as long as we need to handle your enquiry. If we don't end up working together, we delete it within 12 months of our last contact at the latest. If we start working together, we keep it for as long as the relationship lasts and then for the periods required by law.

6. Who we share your data with

We do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. To run the website and handle contact requests, we work with providers that process data on our behalf as data processors:

  • GitHub, Inc., which hosts the website. It is based in the United States and certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which guarantees an adequate level of protection.
  • Google Ireland Limited (Google Workspace), our email provider, which delivers the form data to us and where we receive your messages. Any transfers to the United States are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, or to transfer it (portability). Email us at contacto@easypills.studio saying which right you want to exercise.

If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, and the website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

9. Children

This website is not aimed at children under 14, who should not give us any personal data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the law or in our service. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.
