1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that some websites store in your browser to remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or browsing behaviour.

2. This website does not use cookies

www.easypills.studio does not set its own cookies or third-party cookies. Nor does it use analytics, advertising or embedded social media tools. Fonts, images and video are served from the website itself, with no connections to external services.

That's why we don't show a cookie banner or need your consent for cookies.

3. Links to other websites

If you follow a link to another site, such as LinkedIn, that site may use its own cookies. We recommend checking its cookie policy.

4. How to manage cookies in your browser

In any case, you can view, block or delete cookies in your browser's privacy settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge or others).

5. Changes to this policy

If we add cookies in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it. If you have any questions, email us at contacto@easypills.studio.
